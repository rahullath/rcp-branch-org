# Branch Organizer

A phone app for running a communist branch's week. Everyone in the branch sees what's on, what needs doing and who's doing it, without chasing people in the group chat.

[Try the demo](/login) · [Start your branch](/guide/start-a-branch) · [Run your own copy](/guide/run-your-own)

## What it does

A branch's week runs in the same order every time. The app follows that order and does the remembering.

1. **Plan.** Officers add events: branch meetings, open meetings, paper sales, stalls, socials, reading group sessions. Paper sales suggest the times most people are usually free.
2. **Announce.** A few days before, whoever leads an event gets one card listing what isn't set up yet (place, lead-off, chair, readings, two people going) and a button that copies the announcement for the branch chat.
3. **Invite.** Each event has a public link that explains, in plain words, what the meeting is and what it's about. Members get one card asking whether any of their contacts should come, and send the link from their own WhatsApp.
4. **Remind.** One reminder to people who haven't answered. One nudge the day before to anyone who hasn't done the reading. Nobody gets a list of who hasn't.
5. **Report.** After a sale or stall, whoever was given the report says how many papers sold and who they met.
6. **Follow up.** Tasks from the minutes land with their owners. Each new contact stays with whoever met them. Candidates build a record from what they actually did.

Everything that asks you to do something lands in one **Inbox**, and goes away once it's done.

## The screens

- **Week**: today and the next seven days, and the one thing waiting for you.
- **Events**: everything coming up, each with who's going, the reading, the agenda and what to bring.
- **Inbox**: what's waiting for you, grouped.
- **Contacts**: the people you're working with. Private to you.
- **More**: agenda, education and reading groups, tasks, follow-ups, materials, paper sales, finance, resources.
