# For officers

Everyone gets the same five screens. Officers get a few more things in their inbox and a short list of tools under More.

## Any event lead

- **Set up card** a few days before your event: two days before a sale or stall, four before a branch meeting, a week before an open meeting or social. It lists what's missing and has **Copy for the branch chat**.
- **Call it** if the event needed a number of people by a deadline and hasn't got them.
- **Report** after a sale, stall or reading group session, unless you gave the report to someone else under Roles.

## General secretary

- Members and roles, the join code, and the viewer link (Settings).
- **Candidate review** card a week before each candidate's three months are up, with their record: branch meetings, sales and events, jobs given and done.
- Requests for roles.

## Organiser

- Paper sales: best times from everyone's usual week, **Plan a sale**, spots, other branches' sales nearby.
- Follow-ups: who keeps in touch with whom, and who's gone quiet.

## Education officer

- The branch programme: topics, lead-offs, readings.
- Reading groups: one text each (a book, a pamphlet or one In Defence of Marxism issue), when, where, who for.

## Finance officer

- Finance: income, spending, pledges and totals.

## Social media officer

- Social: the week's posts as Draft, Review, Ready, and the graphic maker.

## Taking minutes

Paste the action points into Agenda → Actions. "Jo and Dan to book the room by Friday" becomes a task in both their inboxes with a due date. People not on the app yet can still be given tasks.
