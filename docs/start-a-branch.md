# Start your branch

These steps are for a copy of the app that someone already runs. To run your own, see [Run your own copy](/guide/run-your-own).

## Three steps

1. **Sign in** with your email. You get a link; open it on your phone.
2. **Start a branch.** Give its name ("Leeds Branch 1") and your city or district. You become its secretary.
3. **Share the join code.** Settings → Branch → Copy invite. Post it in the branch chat. Anyone who opens it signs in and lands in your branch.

On a copy shared by several branches, an admin approves each new branch first. They get a notification; you'll see "Waiting for approval" until then.

## The first week

Doing these once makes everything else work.

- **Ask everyone to set their usual week** (Settings → When you're usually free). Paper sale times are suggested from it. Each member gets one card asking for it.
- **Add your spots** (More → Paper sales → Where we sell). Any "where" field also saves a new spot as you type it. Birmingham branches can add a ready-made list of spots in one tap.
- **Give out roles** (Settings → Members and roles): organiser, education officer, finance officer, paper sales officer, social media officer.
- **Put the next branch meeting in** with its discussion topic, lead-off and a reading.
- **Bring in what you already have.** More → Import gives you a prompt to paste into any chat assistant together with your notes, minutes or spreadsheets. It turns them into contacts, events, tasks, materials and reading groups that you check before they go in.

## Showing someone how your branch runs

Settings → Branch → **Make a viewer link**. Whoever joins with it can look around but can't change anything, and never sees contacts, phone numbers, money, availability or follow-ups. Turn the link off when you're done.
